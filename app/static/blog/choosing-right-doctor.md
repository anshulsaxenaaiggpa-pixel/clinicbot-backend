## 5 Steps to Choose the Perfect Doctor

### 1. **Identify Your Medical Need**

Different specialists handle different conditions:
- **General Physician**: Fever, cold, routine checkups
- **Cardiologist**: Heart conditions, chest pain, blood pressure
- **Dermatologist**: Skin issues, acne, hair loss
- **Orthopedist**: Bone, joint, muscle pain
- **Pediatrician**: Children's health

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Choosing based on price alone** - Low fees don't always mean good care  
❌ **Ignoring reviews** - Patient feedback reveals treatment quality  
❌ **Not verifying credentials** - Always check registration  
❌ **Delaying appointments** - Early treatment prevents complications  

## When to See a Specialist vs General Doctor

**See a General Physician first for**:
- Fever, cold, cough
- Minor infections
- Routine health checkups
- Vaccination

**See a Specialist directly for**:
- Heart chest pain → Cardiologist
- Severe skin issues → Dermatologist
- Broken bones → Orthopedist
- Children's health → Pediatrician
